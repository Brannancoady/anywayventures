01 · TECHNOLOGY, PRODUCT & AI DUE DILIGENCE
LAST UPDATED {{ updated }}

Diligence on what the business is — and a plan for what AI makes it.

Most technical diligence tells you what could go wrong. We cover that properly — then go further, with a costed plan for how AI changes the target's cost base and growth in the first hundred days of ownership. Led by someone who has built, run and sold the kind of business you're buying, with specialist security and data reviewers where the target warrants it.

Book 30 minutes FIXED QUOTE IN 48 HOURS · IC SESSION INSIDE 3 WEEKS

Three strands

Commissioned together or separately. Together is better: the AI plan is only credible if it rests on an honest read of the platform and the product.

TECHNOLOGY

Is the platform what they say it is?

Architecture, code quality, scalability, security and data posture, third-party dependency, and the engineering team — capability, key-person risk, and how they actually ship.

Answers: what breaks at 3× scale, and what it costs to fix.
PRODUCT

Does the product earn the growth story?

Usage and retention behind the headline numbers, roadmap realism, competitive position, pricing power, and whether product leadership can take it to the next stage.

Answers: whether the plan in the model is buildable.
AI VALUE CREATION

What does AI make this business worth?

A function-by-function plan of attack: where AI takes cost out, where it accelerates revenue, what the data and systems will support, and what it costs to deliver. Plus a hard look at any AI claims the target already makes.

Answers: the upside you can underwrite, and when it lands.
THE NEW ANGLE

AI diligence is a value-creation plan, not a risk register

Every buyer now assumes AI is part of the thesis. Very few reach completion knowing where, how much, or how fast. This closes that gap before the price is set.

Four ways in

Most engagements start with the first and grow into the others. All fixed price against a defined outcome.

01 · EMBEDDED SPRINT

Two to three weeks inside the business

We sit with the people doing the work — ops, finance, sales, support — and map where the hours and the errors actually go. By the end of the sprint the top one or two are automated and running, not specified.

You leave with: working software, a ranked and costed backlog, and a team that has watched it be built.
02 · INTERNAL TOOLING

The software the roadmap keeps deferring

Every business has three internal tools it has wanted for two years — the pricing calculator, the ops dashboard, the thing that kills the spreadsheet. Built properly on your stack, with AI where it earns its place.

You leave with: production software, documentation, and engineers able to extend it.
03 · AUTOMATION & AGENTS

Cost out of the operation, permanently

Quoting, onboarding, reconciliation, reporting, support triage, document handling. Agents built against your systems and your data, with a human in the loop wherever judgement or liability sits.

You leave with: a measured before-and-after on hours, cost and error rate.
04 · CAPABILITY TRANSFER

Your team, AI-native for real

You bought the licences and nothing changed. We work alongside your engineers and operators until the workflows they use daily are the ones with AI in them — then deliberately make ourselves unnecessary.

You leave with: habits and workflows that survive our departure. Measured at six months.

How an engagement runs

No rate card, no juniors, no hand-off once the pitch is won. If we finish faster we earn a better margin; if we underestimate, we absorb it.

Founder, operator, investor — and still the one writing the code.

I co-founded Netsells in York and ran it as chief executive for a decade, building digital products for Trainline, Coca-Cola, the BBC and Toolstation, and growing it into one of the UK's better-regarded product consultancies. Anyway Ventures is the single vehicle for all of it: my capital, my board work, and the thing that now takes most of my week — going into businesses and portfolios and building AI into how they run, with the people who'll be left with it.
